Prize Rules

Last updated 4 November 2026

Apple is not a sponsor of, and is not involved in, this prize contest in any way.

  1. Sponsor. [YOUR NAME], [ADDRESS]. Rewards are provided by participating restaurants.
  2. Entry. Free. Nothing needs to be bought. Anyone with an account who meets these rules enters by uploading a photo. Open to UK residents aged 13 or over, excluding the sponsor and the restaurant providing a reward.
  3. Period. Each week, ending Sunday at about 22:15 UK time.
  4. How winners are chosen. Votes score a photo: Bonus Yes 5, Yes 3, No 1. Scores count all votes since the photo was posted, so the same photo can win more than once. The uploader of the top photo Overall wins one Premium reward. The uploaders of the top Restaurant and top Recipe photos each win one Secondary reward, unless they’ve already won that week’s Premium reward. The reward itself is chosen at random from those available, never one the winner provided. Photos hidden or removed by moderation can’t win.
  5. Prizes. Each prize is a QR code reward set by the restaurant, shown in My Won Codes. There’s no cash alternative, it can’t be transferred, and it must be used by its expiry date. If no reward is available, none is awarded.
  6. Winners. Winners are told in the app. A winner’s display name is shared with the restaurant providing the reward and appears on the leaderboards.
  7. Fair play. Anyone who manipulates votes, uses several accounts or breaks the Terms of Use may be disqualified.
  8. Restaurants. Restaurants are responsible for honouring their rewards.
