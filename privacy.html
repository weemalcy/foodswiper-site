Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

I Feel Like Food is run by [YOUR NAME] ("we"), [ADDRESS]. Contact: [SUPPORT EMAIL].

What we collect

  • Account details: your username, display name, a password (stored only in hashed form), your account type, and which version of our terms you accepted.
  • Sign in with Apple: if you link it, an identifier from Apple. We don’t ask Apple for your name or email.
  • Photos and details you post: photos, descriptions, cuisine, restaurant or dish names and addresses.
  • Location: your device’s precise location, with your permission, to find photos near you and to tag uploads. It’s sent to find photos but only stored with a photo you upload. If you pick a restaurant, the photo uses the restaurant’s location instead.
  • Activity: your votes, photos you report and your reasons, people you block, leaderboard positions, prizes and notifications.
  • Preferences: distance, dietary and category settings.
  • Restaurants: reward codes and details you add.

The app removes location and other metadata from a photo’s file before upload, unless it can’t process that image format, in which case the original file is sent. We don’t use your data for advertising, sell it, or track you across other companies’ apps.

How we use it

To run the app: showing photos nearby, counting votes, running leaderboards and prizes, keeping accounts secure, and keeping the app safe. Text you post is checked automatically for abusive language, and a photo reported by several people is hidden until we review it. Who can see it

  • Other users see your photos and their details, and your display name on leaderboards. People you block, and people who block you, don’t see each other’s photos. Nobody is told they’ve been blocked.
  • Restaurants see the display name of anyone who wins their reward.
  • Our admins can see accounts, photos and reports in order to moderate the service.
  • Service providers: Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and photo storage in London, UK. Apple provides Sign in with Apple, and Apple Maps answers postcode searches, which your device sends directly to Apple. These providers must protect your data to at least the standard in this policy.

We may also share information where the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data until you delete your account. To delete it, go to My Profile > Delete Account. This removes your account, photos, votes, blocks, notifications and unwon reward codes straight away. If you’ve won a prize, the restaurant’s record of it stays, without your name. Prize records are kept for [PERIOD]. You can turn off location access at any time in iOS Settings.

An admin may remove your account or photos for breaking our Terms of Use.
