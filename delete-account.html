Delete your account

Last updated 15 November 2026

You can delete your I Feel Like Food account and its data at any time, on iPhone, iPad or Android.

In the app

  1. Open I Feel Like Food and log in.
  2. Tap My Profile.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account, then confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away and you’re logged out.

If you can’t use the app

Email weemalcy@hotmail.com from any address with your username and “Delete my account”. We’ll check the account is yours, delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What’s deleted

  • Your account: username, display name, password and preferences.
  • Your photos and their descriptions, your votes, the people you’ve blocked, your reports and your notifications.
  • Any reward codes your restaurant added that haven’t been won.

What’s kept

If you’ve won a prize, or your restaurant’s reward has been won, the record of that prize stays without your name, for 6 months. See the Privacy Policy.
