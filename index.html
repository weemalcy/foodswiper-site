Support

I Feel Like Food is a free iPhone and iPad app for discovering food near you: swipe through photos from people nearby, vote on them, and climb the weekly leaderboards for a chance to win rewards from restaurants.

Contact us

Email weemalcy@hotmail.com. We aim to reply within two working days, and we review reports of abusive content within 24 hours.

Report or block

Tap the flag on any photo to report it or block whoever posted it. A photo reported by several people is hidden until we review it. To unblock someone, go to My Profile > Blocked Users.

Delete your account

Go to My Profile > Delete Account in the app. This deletes your account and your data straight away.
